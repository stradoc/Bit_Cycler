## Decisions

Technology and design decisions, with the reason for each (`RULES.md` rule 3). A decision that changed an earlier one says what it replaces.

### Technology

| Date | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-28 | Rendering: **Phaser 3.90** (a move to Phaser 4 possible later) | A full framework with built-in WebGL effects (bloom, barrel) and a pixel-art mode; far more docs and examples than Phaser 4, which matters in a one-month project |

| 2026-10-28 | **No WebAssembly** | The simulation is trivial to compute; wasm would add a toolchain and debugging cost for nothing |

### The music follows the player
- **Why** (user): make the music "interactive with the gameplay"; "if you miss the beat, it should skip or stall the sound".
- **The rule** (`song/groove.ts`, new, pure): the song moves on one beat for each cycle that does work (an issue, or the pipeline filling and emptying), so every lost cycle is a beat the song loses: a sloppy run makes the song itself longer, the CPI by ear. How a lost beat sounds says why: a needed stall **holds** (the bass waits on the chord, the hats tick); the player's mistake (a miss, the wrong key, off the beat, a stall for nothing) **skips** like a scratched CD (the last sixteenth stuttered); a wrong branch guess or an early issue **stops like tape** (the chord diving in pitch). Before guessing (tiers 2-3) every taken branch flushes, the hardware's cost, so it holds instead of a tape stop every loop. The music reacts one beat after the cycle (its beat is scheduled before the press is judged), right after the miss's own buzz.
- **The sounds** (`audio/music.ts`): hold, skip and tape stop; a memory wait now hangs on the chord as a quiet drone under the ticks (the waiting cue's low buzz went); key presses play notes of the chord sounding (MEM the root, ALU the third, BRANCH the fifth, low to high as before; `hit-*` cues take a pitch); the song ends on its home chord with a crash (at most 1.8 s, as the replay starts 2 s after). Wired in `PipelineScene` (`musicBeat`, the cycles since the last beat); replays reproduce it from the recorded run.
- Tests: 174 (`tests/groove.test.ts`, new: what each kind of cycle does, several in a beat, practice waiting). Headless Chrome, logging each beat the music plays: tier 2 perfect (two holds at each load-use hazard, a hold at each taken branch), no keys (the song stuck, skipping), tier 1 and tier 4 perfect (straight through), each ending on the home chord. Exported: `sound-export/music/reactions/` (hold, skip, flush, memory-wait, ending); the export's README (written by `tools/export-sounds.mjs`) lists them. No page errors.
- **Not checked: how it sounds and feels.** Whether the reactions read clearly, whether holding twice per loop at tier 2 is too halting, and whether the hit notes sit well over the melody: someone has to play it with sound.
